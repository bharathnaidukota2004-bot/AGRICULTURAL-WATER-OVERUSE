About the Project

This project aims to address one of the most pressing issues in agriculture—overuse of water resources. By integrating Nash Equilibrium principles with Machine Learning models, our system predicts water requirements more accurately and promotes smarter irrigation practices. The goal is to balance productivity with sustainability, ensuring that farmers and the environment both benefit.

Our Approach

Nash Equilibrium

Game-theory-based strategies that ensure optimal water distribution among competing agricultural demands.

Machine Learning Models

Advanced algorithms to predict crop water needs based on soil, weather, and crop data.

Sustainability Impact

Reducing water waste while maintaining crop yields, leading to long-term agricultural resilience.
